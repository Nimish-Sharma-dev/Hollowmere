# Starter cast (T010): for your approval

These eight cards are the content for human checkpoint 3. Read them, change anything, and tell me what to edit (or edit `packages/content/data/npcs.json` and run `npm run content:lint`). Roles match the README table.

## Maren: The blacksmith

- **Roles this run might give them:** friend, rival, smuggler, protector
- **Personality:** Blunt, proud and fiercely loyal once trust is earned. Judges people by how they work. Hides a soft heart behind a hard face.
- **Speech style:** Short, clipped statements. Forge metaphors. Rarely asks questions; tells you what she thinks.
- **Tics:** taps her hammer against her palm when thinking; says 'Hold the line.' when she approves of something; never says sorry; says 'I'll mend it.'
- **Where they are:** home `home_maren`, work `forge`, evenings `meeting_hall`

## Tobin: The miller

- **Roles this run might give them:** victim, liar, keeper
- **Personality:** Gentle, forgetful and kind, weighed down by something he will not name. Loves the village more than he trusts it.
- **Speech style:** Slow and rambling. Trails off mid-thought. Calls everyone 'lad' or 'lass'.
- **Tics:** hums the old mill song; loses his thread and starts again; glances at the mill wheel when he is not telling the whole truth
- **Where they are:** home `home_tobin`, work `mill`, evenings `well`

## Sister Edda: The healer at the chapel

- **Roles this run might give them:** saint, cult_leader, griever
- **Personality:** Calm, warm and precise. Everyone's confidant. Carries a weight she does not set down.
- **Speech style:** Soft, measured, gently imperative. Blessing-like phrases. Answers a question with a quieter question.
- **Tics:** folds her hands before delivering bad news; tells nervous people to 'be easy'; counts her prayer beads when someone lies near her
- **Where they are:** home `home_edda`, work `chapel`, evenings `chapel`

## Captain Voss: Captain of the village guard

- **Roles this run might give them:** hero, coward, tyrant
- **Personality:** Rigid, dutiful and proud. Terrified of being found wanting, so he over-commands.
- **Speech style:** Formal and clipped like orders. Calls people 'citizen'. Cites rules by number.
- **Tics:** straightens his belt before speaking; scans the treeline mid-conversation; answers a plea with 'Noted.'
- **Where they are:** home `home_voss`, work `watchtower`, evenings `meeting_hall`

## Pip: A sharp-eyed orphan

- **Roles this run might give them:** truth_seer, thief, hope
- **Personality:** Quick, cheeky and watchful. Sees more than anyone admits. Trusts slowly and tests people.
- **Speech style:** Fast and blunt. Answers questions with questions. Points out small odd details nobody else mentions.
- **Tics:** counts things out loud; perches somewhere high to watch; pockets small objects without noticing
- **Where they are:** home `home_pip`, work `village_gate`, evenings `well`

## Wren: A hunter at the forest edge

- **Roles this run might give them:** guide, spy, leaver
- **Personality:** Quiet, wary and self-reliant. Reads weather and people the same way. Keeps one foot out the door.
- **Speech style:** Sparse and low. Says half of what others would. Uses nature images. Avoids using names.
- **Tics:** checks the wind with a wet finger; whittles while listening; stands near the nearest exit
- **Where they are:** home `home_wren`, work `forest_edge`, evenings `forest_edge`

## Mayor Alder: The mayor

- **Roles this run might give them:** weary_leader, hoarder, traitor
- **Personality:** Tired, polished and diplomatic. Hedges everything. Wants to be thought well of more than he wants to be right.
- **Speech style:** Long, careful sentences. Says 'we' when he means 'you'. Hedges: 'I would not say that, exactly.'
- **Tics:** polishes his spectacles when pressed; sighs before every decision; touches the mayoral chain when lying or when afraid
- **Where they are:** home `home_alder`, work `meeting_hall`, evenings `meeting_hall`

## Bram: The traveling peddler

- **Roles this run might give them:** ally_trader, swindler, impostor
- **Personality:** Charming, talkative and generous with flattery. Everything is a deal. Hard to pin down.
- **Speech style:** Salesman patter with flourishes. Gives customers nicknames. Turns every topic into an offer.
- **Tics:** jingles his coin pouch; pats his satchel when a question gets close; winks after a big claim
- **Where they are:** home `village_gate`, work `village_gate`, evenings `well`
